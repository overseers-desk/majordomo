---
name: events
description: Someone asks what's on, what is happening, things to do, or about events, markets, shows or festivals nearby or in a named place, on given days.
---

Use WebSearch to find events, and WebFetch to confirm a date or time on the event's own page when a search result is unclear or out of date.

Place: "nearby" means the location in your system prompt unless the person names another. Work out the exact days asked (today, this weekend, Saturday) from the message time before searching.

Answer with a short list. For each event give the name, date and time, place, and a link. Include only events that fall on the days asked; drop anything whose date you could not confirm or that is a recurring listing with no sign it runs that day.

If nothing reliable turns up, say so plainly rather than padding the list; offer to look at a different day or place.

Send the answer with `reply`, in the person's language.
